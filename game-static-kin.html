Overview

Static Kin is a tiny horror game made entirely on the PICO-8 fantasy console. You're alone in a cabin, tuning a radio through bands of static to keep something outside calm — tune too fast or too slow and it notices. Built as a one-week experiment in working within PICO-8's strict technical limits: a 128x128 screen and a 16-color palette.

What I worked on

  • Everything — solo project, code, pixel art, and chiptune audio
  • A tension system driven entirely by how precisely you tune the radio
  • Squeezing the whole game under PICO-8's cart size limit

Postmortem notes

Working inside PICO-8's constraints forced smaller, sharper decisions than a full engine would have. Replace this with your own thoughts on working within hard technical limits, or what you'd build next with them.
