Overview

Hollow Ledger is a narrative game about a debt collector working a town where memories are the currency — every debt is paid in a piece of someone's past. You decide which memories are worth taking, and live with what that does to the people you take them from.

What I'm building

  • A branching dialogue system in Ink, wired into Unity for playtesting
  • A memory-value system that tracks how debts change character relationships
  • Static art and placeholder VO to test pacing before investing in final art

Where it stands

Currently a text-and-static-art prototype focused on proving the dialogue branching holds together over a full story arc. Replace this with your own current milestone, or what you're testing next.
