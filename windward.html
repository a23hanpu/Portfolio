Windward

Shipped

Group Project · Unity · Released 2025

Unity

Overview

A story driven game where the player uses a ship to explore different islands and help the citizens with tasks as you slowly learn more about the world.

This game was made for a game project course during my second year at the University of Skövde where the goal was to create a game in a group of 10 (?) people from start to finish in 12 weeks.
I was one of three programmers and was responsible for the boat and player movement, minigames, menu logic and I also worked on some accessibility features such as automatically changing icons and smooth transitions when switching between different types of controls.

What I worked on

  • Programming for the boat and player movement
  • Minigames
  • All menu logic
  • Accessibility and QOL features
