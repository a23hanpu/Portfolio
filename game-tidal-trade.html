Overview

Tidal Trade is a cozy trading-route sim where you captain a small boat between coastal towns, timing your runs around tides and weather to keep cargo dry and customers happy. Built as a university capstone project with a team of five over one semester.

What I worked on

  • Led gameplay programming for the shipping-and-weather system
  • Designed the tide-timing mechanic that routes are built around
  • Coordinated with our artist and audio lead on how systems surfaced in the UI

Postmortem notes
