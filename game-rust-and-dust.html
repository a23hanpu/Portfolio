Overview

Rust & Dust is a top-down salvage-and-survive game set in a city that's been half-swallowed by sand. You scavenge parts by day and defend your camp from what the dust storms drag in by night. We're aiming for something between a survival crafting game and a tight arena shooter.

What I'm working on

  • Combat feel — hit-stop, camera shake, weapon weight
  • Enemy AI, including pack behavior for the "dust hound" enemies
  • Tuning the day/night threat curve alongside the designer
