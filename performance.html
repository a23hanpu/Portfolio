PROJECT 01

DOTS vs Standard Unity Performance Comparison

In this project, I compared the performance of Standard Unity, Unity with multithreading, and Unity with DOTS (Data-Oriented Technology Stack).

To make this comparison I created a scene with 2D spheres that bounce around the screen and used PairDetection to check for collision, and when they collided with each other I colored them red.

Results

The results were logged into a CSV file for analysis but you can also see the clear difference in performance in the videos attached.
