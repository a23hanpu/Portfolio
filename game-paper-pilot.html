Overview

Paper Pilot is a one-button flying game made in 48 hours for a game jam themed "fold." You control a paper plane by folding and unfolding its wings mid-air — folded, you dive fast and thin through gaps; unfolded, you glide slow and wide. Wind gusts try to catch you off guard either way.

What I worked on

  • Everything — solo jam entry, code, art, and the three-track soundtrack
  • Procedural wind-gust generation tuned during the last six hours of the jam
  • A shareable results screen so people could compare distances

Postmortem notes

Placed top 20 out of 400+ entries. The one-button control scheme was the whole pitch — replace this with what you learned building under a jam deadline, or what you'd expand if you revisited it.
